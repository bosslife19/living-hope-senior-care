Information for caregivers

Why caregivers choose Living Hope

We take care of the people who take care of our clients.

Flexible scheduling

Part-time and full-time shifts with schedules built around your availability.

A supportive team

You'll have a responsive office team and nurse-led guidance whenever you need it.

Meaningful work

One-on-one care where you build real relationships and see the difference you make.

Work close to home

We match caregivers with clients in their own community whenever possible.

Learn and grow

Learn from a founder with over 20 years of nursing experience.

Respect and appreciation

Your work matters, and we make sure you know it.

Who we're looking for

Caring people who take pride in their work

Experience helps, but character matters most.

  • Patient, warm and respectful with older adults
  • Reliable, punctual and able to work independently
  • Clear communicator with clients, families and our team
  • Experience in caregiving or home care is a plus
  • Reliable transportation (for transportation and errand services)
Hiring requirements

Documents we'll need from caregivers

Have these ready to speed up your hiring. You can tell us your status for each one in the application and upload documents if you have them.

HCA registration

Home Care Aide (HCA) registration or certification, current and in good standing.

Current TB test

A recent tuberculosis (TB) test result showing you are clear to work.

Background clearance

A completed background check clearance for working with vulnerable adults.

Proof of authorization to work in the U.S.

Documentation that you are authorized to work in the United States.
