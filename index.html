Welcome to Living Hope

Helping seniors live well, right where they belong.

For most seniors, there is no place like home. Our purpose is to make staying there possible, safely and comfortably.

Living Hope Senior Care provides non-medical, in-home care for older adults and peace of mind for the families who love them. Our caregivers help with daily routines, meals, errands and companionship, always with patience, kindness and respect for each person's independence.

  • Care tailored to each senior
  • Caregivers who treat clients like family
  • Clear communication with families
  • Flexible support as needs change

Meet our founder

Kathy Ruiz Alarcon, Founder & CEO

“Every senior deserves to be cared for the way we would care for our own family.”

Kathy brings more than 20 years of experience in nursing to Living Hope Senior Care. Throughout her career, she has cared for patients and families through some of life's most important moments, and she has seen how much comfort, dignity and familiar surroundings matter to recovery and wellbeing.

She founded Living Hope to give seniors the option to age safely at home, supported by caregivers who bring both skill and genuine compassion. Her nursing background shapes how we plan care, train our team and communicate with families.
